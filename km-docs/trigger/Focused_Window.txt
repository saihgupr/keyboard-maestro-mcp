====== Focused Window Trigger ======

The //Focused Window trigger// (v7.0+) executes the macro when the selected option occurs.

{{:trigger:km-focused-window-trigger.png?nolink|}}

**The Options are:**
  - The focused window changes.
  - The focused window title changes.
    * Occurs when **either**:
      * The front window **changes** (to a window with a different title)\\  **or**
      * The **title** of the front window changes.
  - The focused window’s title changes.
    * Occurs **only** when the **title** of the Front Window changes.
  - The focused window’s frame changes.
