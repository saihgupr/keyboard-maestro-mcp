====== DPI Functions ======

The DPI(X|Y) and SOURCEDPI(X|Y) functions return the horizontal or vertical DPI of the images that the action is operating on in actions like the [Resize Image](action:Resize_Image) or the [Composite onto Image](action: Composite_onto_Image) actions.
