====== Audio Device Tokens ======

The audio device tokens (v11.0+) return the name or UID of the currently selected audio input, output or sound effects devices.

The tokens are:

* %AudioInputDevice%
* %AudioInputDeviceUID%
* %AudioOutputDevice% (available since v8.0)
* %AudioOutputDeviceUID%
* %AudioSoundEffectsDevice%
* %AudioSoundEffectsDeviceUID%
