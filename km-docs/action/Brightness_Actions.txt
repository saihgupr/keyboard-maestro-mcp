====== Screen Brightness Actions ======

The //Increase Screen Brightness// and //Decrease Screen Brightness// actions increase or decrease the screen brightness by one unit (one unit is 1/16th of the total range). 

The //Small Increase Screen Brightness// and //Small Decrease Screen Brightness// actions (v9.0+) increase or decrease the screen brightness by one quarter of a unit.

When you use these actions the system brightness overlay briefly appears on the screen.

These actions are the same as pressing the hardware brightness buttons, with or without the option&shift key.  They typically affect the primary screen only.
