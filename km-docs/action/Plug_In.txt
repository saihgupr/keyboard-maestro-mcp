====== Third Party Plug In ======

A Third Party Plug In action allows users to write and [contribute](https://forum.keyboardmaestro.com/c/plugin) custom actions based on scripts.

See the [documentation](/manual/Plug_In_Actions) for more information on writing plug in actions.
