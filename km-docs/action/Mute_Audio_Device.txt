====== Mute Audio Device ======

The //Mute Audio Device// actions (v11.0+) let you mute, unmute or toggle mute of the default audio device, the sound effects device, or a specific audio device.

{{ mute-audio-device-action.png?nolink&674x88 | Mute Audio Device Action }}

You can select from available devices in the popup menu on the right of the field.
