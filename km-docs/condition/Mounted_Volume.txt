The //Mounted Volume condition// looks for a mounted volume with name that:

* exactly matches a specified string.
* contains (case insensitively) a specified string.
* matches a specified [[:Regular Expressions|regular expression]].

and then checks whether it:

* is mounted.
* is not mounted.
