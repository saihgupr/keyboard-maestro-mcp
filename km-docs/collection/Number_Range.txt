The //Number Range collection// lists numbers in a range, including the start and end number, counting either upwards or downwards.

As with all [numeric fields](:Text_Fields), the numbers can be calculations including [functions](:Functions), and the fields expand to allow for longer expressions as soon as you type something other than a number.
