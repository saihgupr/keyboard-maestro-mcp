==== Lines In collection ====

//Lines In collection// is contained in the [[action:For_Each|For Each]] action.

//Lines In collection// allows each line from a specified source to be processed.

Lines may be read from the System Clipboard, from a Named Clipboard, from a variable, or from a file.

It is also possible to include or exclude blank lines.
