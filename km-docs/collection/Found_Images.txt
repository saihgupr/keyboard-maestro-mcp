====== Found Images Collection ======

The //Found Images [[:Collections|collection]]// lists all the locations that the image matches on the screen (v7.0+).

You can order the matches by best matches first, top to bottom or left to right.

The result, stored in the [[action:For_Each| For Each action ]] variable is the rectangle containing the match followed by the fuzz of the match (0-100), ie, (left,top,width,height,fuzz).
